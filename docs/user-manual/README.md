# DCEL Office Suite — Staff Instruction Manual & User Guide

> **Company:** Dewatering Construction Etc. Limited (DCEL)  
> **App Version:** v1.5.3+  
> **Who is this for?** Site Supervisors, Field Engineers, HR Officers, Accountants, Mechanics, and General Staff  

---

## 🌟 Welcome to Your Work App

**DCEL Office Suite** is the everyday business app for **Dewatering Construction Etc. Limited**. It brings everything you need to run daily operations into one easy-to-use system:

- **For Field Staff & Site Foremen:** Mark site attendance, submit machine run-hours, log diesel refills, request emergency site funds, and fill out the daily site diary.
- **For HR & Admin:** Manage staff profiles, approve leave requests, register healthcare insurance (HMO), and handle employee onboarding.
- **For Accounts & Finance:** Run monthly payroll in minutes, create client invoices, record client bank payments, and track company expenses.
- **For Engineers & Project Managers:** Trace blueprints in the DewaterCAD simulator, place pumps and header lines automatically, calculate water flow rates, and create transit waybills.

## 💡 Quick Tips for Daily Use

1. **Working on Mobile on Remote Sites:**  
   You can open the app on your phone even when you are on a remote site with low signal. You can log attendance and fuel normally, and the app will sync automatically once you are back in network range.
2. **Finding Anything Fast:**  
   Press `Ctrl + /` on your computer keyboard at any time to open the instant search bar. You can type any worker’s name, client name, equipment tag, or invoice number to jump straight to it.
3. **Switching Screen Modes:**  
   Click the **Moon/Sun** icon at the top right of the screen to switch between clean white mode and dark mode for night work.
