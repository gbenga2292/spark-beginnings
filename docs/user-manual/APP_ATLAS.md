# DCEL Office Suite — Visual Screen Map & Navigation Guide

> **Company:** Dewatering Construction Etc. Limited (DCEL)  
> **Document Type:** Non-Technical Screen Directory & Menu Guide  
> **For:** All Office Staff, Site Supervisors, Engineers, and Managers  

---

## 🧭 How Navigation Works in DCEL Office Suite

DCEL Office Suite organizes all company tools into **9 clear departments** located in the sidebar menu on the left side of your screen.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🏠 Dashboard        ➔ Your daily overview and task list     │
│ 🏢 Clients          ➔ Client profiles, contacts, and hours  │
│ 💬 Communication    ➔ Tasks, chat, reminders, and minutes   │
│ 🛠️ Tools            ➔ Dewatering calculators & simulators   │
│ 👥 HR               ➔ Staff directory, attendance & leaves  │
│ 📦 Operations       ➔ Equipment, diesel, waybills & repairs │
│ 💰 Accounts         ➔ Invoices, payroll, expenses & budget  │
│ 📁 Reports          ➔ Summary sheets and printable exports  │
│ ⚙️ Settings         ➔ Users, passwords, and system rules    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Click any department in the sidebar to open its list of screens.
- Click the **"← Back to All Categories"** arrow at the top of the menu anytime to switch departments.
- If you need a wider view on a laptop screen, click the **Collapse Menu** icon (`⇥`) at the top left.

## 📋 Complete List of Screens & What You Can Do There

### 1. Dashboard (Main Hub)
- **Dashboard (`/tasks/dashboard`):** See your active tasks, urgent company reminders, and quick progress numbers for the week.

### 2. Clients & Site Reconciliation
- **Client 360 (`/client-360`):** View a client’s full profile—their active construction sites, machines currently working for them, and payment status.
- **Phonebook (`/clients/phonebook`):** Company contact directory. You can search phone numbers or take a picture of a paper business card to automatically save the contact.
- **Machine Recon (`/operations/machine-reconciliation`):** Compare the machine hours logged on site with the hours approved by the client for billing.
- **Site Analytics (`/operations/site-analytics`):** Simple charts showing which equipment is working, which is idle, and how much diesel each site is using.

### 3. Communication & Daily Field Records
- **Task Register (`/tasks`):** See all your assigned jobs in a List or Board view. Add comments, attach photos, and check off completed subtasks.
- **Task Reminders (`/tasks/reminders`):** Set pop-up reminders for yourself or your team so important deadlines are never forgotten.
- **External Comms (`/comm-log`):** Keep a record of letters, approvals, and emails sent to or received from clients and government agencies.
- **Daily Journal (`/daily-journal`):** A daily work diary for site supervisors to record what was done today, what went well, and what was delayed.
- **Meeting Minutes (`/minutes`):** Type up meeting notes, assign action items to attendees, and sign digitally right on screen.
- **Site Requests (`/operations/site-requests`):** Site engineers can request urgent cash, tools, diesel, or spare parts from the head office.

### 4. Technical & Engineering Tools
- **DewaterCAD Simulator (`/operations/simulator`):** Draw or upload a site drawing, trace your excavation pit, and let the software place dewatering pumps, pipes, and hoses for you.
- **Dewatering Calculator (`/operations/dewatering-calculator`):** Quickly calculate water flow rates, required pump capacity, and water drawdown.
- **Logistics Estimator (`/operations/estimator`):** Calculate truck haulage, mobilization costs, and fuel needed before quoting a job.
- **VAT & Tax Calculator (`/operations/vat-calculator`):** A quick calculator for 7.5% VAT and 5% Withholding Tax on quotes and invoices.

### 5. Human Resources (HR)
- **HR Dashboard (`/hr-dashboard`):** At-a-glance staff count, today’s attendance rate, who is on leave, and hospital insurance status.
- **Daily Attendance (`/attendance`):** Mark daily site registers for day and night shifts, Sunday work, and overtime hours.
- **Employees (`/employees`):** Staff list with photos, phone numbers, emergency contacts, job titles, and bank account numbers.
- **Onboarding (`/onboarding`):** Register new employees, automatically generate their employment contracts, and handle exit clearances.
- **Leaves (`/leaves`):** Apply for annual holiday, sick leave, or emergency leave, and view your remaining vacation days.
- **Salary Loans (`/salary-loans`):** Apply for short-term salary advances and track monthly repayments.
- **HMO Healthcare (`/hmo`):** Check health insurance hospital lists and register spouses and children for company medical cover.
- **Evaluations (`/evaluations`):** Complete bi-annual employee performance reviews.
- **Interviews (`/interviews`):** Manage job applicants and drag-and-drop resumes for automatic reading.
- **Performance & Conduct (`/performance-conduct`):** Keep records of formal employee queries, warnings, and positive commendations.

### 6. Operations, Equipment & Fuel
- **Operations Overview (`/operations`):** Summary of where all company equipment is currently working.
- **Equipment Inventory (`/operations/assets`):** Complete catalog of pumps, generators, pipes, and tools with QR codes and location tags.
- **Waybills (`/operations/waybills`):** Create delivery waybills when moving machines or pipes to a site, complete with driver names and signatures.
- **Quick Checkout (`/operations/checkout`):** Scan a tool barcode to borrow it for site work.
- **Maintenance (`/operations/maintenance`):** Track machine oil changes, servicing schedules, and breakdown repairs.
- **Diesel Refill (`/operations/diesel`):** Log fuel tanker deliveries into storage tanks and daily liters poured into generators and pumps.
- **Vehicles (`/operations/vehicles`):** Check truck and pickup roadworthiness dates, insurance renewals, and driver assignments.
- **Sites (`/operations/sites`):** List of all active and completed project sites.

### 7. Accounts & Finance
- **Client Accounts (`/client-accounts`):** Create and send client invoices for machine rentals and dewatering jobs; log client bank transfers.
- **Payroll (`/payroll`):** Automatically calculate monthly salaries based on attendance, overtime, tax, and pension, then print payslips.
- **Beneficiaries (`/beneficiaries`):** Pay casual site labor, temporary workers, and suppliers who are not on monthly salaries.
- **General Ledger (`/ledger`):** Company cash flow records, official income, and daily site expenses with receipt photos.
- **Bank Statement Import (`/bank-import`):** Upload your bank statement PDF to automatically match payments against client invoices.
- **Vendor Invoices (`/vendor-invoices`):** Record supplier bills for parts, filters, and safety gear awaiting payment approval.
- **Budget (`/budget`):** Track department spending limits against actual spending so projects stay on budget.

### 8. Reports
- **HR Reports (`/reports`):** Printable attendance summaries, staff lists, and leave balances.
- **Financial Reports (`/financial-reports`):** Revenue by site, overdue client invoices, and tax schedules.
- **Task Reports (`/tasks/reports`):** See how fast tasks are being completed across departments.
- **Weekly Executive Report (`/weekly-report`):** A clean summary for directors every Monday morning covering sites, hours, diesel, and money.

### 9. Settings
- **User Accounts (`/users`):** Manage login accounts and control which screens each staff member can see.
- **Settings (`/settings`):** Company official address, current tax rates, and default monthly workdays.
- **Activity Log (`/activity-log`):** A history log showing who created, edited, or deleted records.
