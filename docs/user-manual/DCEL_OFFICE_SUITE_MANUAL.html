🧭 How Navigation Works in DCEL Office Suite

DCEL Office Suite organizes all company tools into 9 clear departments located in the sidebar menu on the left side of your screen.

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🏠 Dashboard        ➔ Your daily overview and task list     │
│ 🏢 Clients          ➔ Client profiles, contacts, and hours  │
│ 💬 Communication    ➔ Tasks, chat, reminders, and minutes   │
│ 🛠️ Tools            ➔ Dewatering calculators & simulators   │
│ 👥 HR               ➔ Staff directory, attendance & leaves  │
│ 📦 Operations       ➔ Equipment, diesel, waybills & repairs │

│ 📁 Reports          ➔ Summary sheets and printable exports  │
│ ⚙️ Settings         ➔ Users, passwords, and system rules    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  • Click any department in the sidebar to open its list of screens.
  • Click the "← Back to All Categories" arrow at the top of the menu anytime to switch departments.
  • If you need a wider view on a laptop screen, click the Collapse Menu icon (⇥) at the top left.

📋 Complete List of Screens & What You Can Do There

1. Dashboard (Main Hub)

  • Dashboard (/tasks/dashboard): See your active tasks, urgent company reminders, and quick progress numbers for the week.

  • Client 360 (/client-360): View a client’s full profile—their active construction sites, machines currently working for them, and payment status.

  • Task Register (/tasks): See all your assigned jobs in a List or Board view. Add comments, attach photos, and check off completed subtasks.

  • DewaterCAD Simulator (/operations/simulator): Draw or upload a site drawing, trace your excavation pit, and let the software place dewatering pumps, pipes, and hoses for you.

  • Logistics Estimator (/operations/estimator): Calculate truck haulage, mobilization costs, and fuel needed before quoting a job.
  • VAT & Tax Calculator (/operations/vat-calculator): A quick calculator for 7.5% VAT and 5% Withholding Tax on quotes and invoices.

2.1 Daily Site Attendance (How to Mark the Daily Register)

Where to go: In the left menu, click HR ➔ Daily Register (/attendance)

The Daily Register records which workers were on duty each day. The system uses this exact information at the end of the month to calculate salaries and overtime pay.

What the Columns Mean:

  • Present: Check the box if the worker reported to work. Leave it unchecked if absent.
  • Day Site / Night Site: Choose the site where the worker was stationed from the dropdown menu (e.g., Eko Atlantic, Dangote, Lekki Phase 1, or Head Office).
  • Shift: Choose whether they worked the Day, Night, or Both (Double Shift).
  • OT (Overtime): Number of extra hours worked beyond their normal shift. Working on Sundays or public holidays automatically counts towards overtime pay.
  • Next Day? (NDW): Check this box if the worker is scheduled to work again tomorrow so the site supervisor knows they are returning.

Step-by-Step: How to Fill the Register

  1. Go to HR ➔ Daily Register.
  2. Select the date using the date box at the top.
  3. Check the Present box for each worker who is on site today.
  4. Select their site location.
  5. If someone worked extra hours, type the hours into the OT box.
  6. Click the blue Save Attendance button at the bottom of the page.

3.1 Understanding How Monthly Payroll Works

Where to go: In the left menu, click Accounts ➔ Payroll (/payroll)

The payroll system in DCEL Office Suite does all the math automatically at the end of every month. It combines employee salary agreements with daily attendance records.

How an Employee's Pay Is Calculated:

   [ Base Salary (pro-rated by days worked) ]
+  [ Allowances: Housing, Transport, Utility ]
+  [ Overtime Earnings (Sunday shifts & extra hours) ]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
=  GROSS EARNINGS
-  [ Pension Savings (8% saved for your retirement) ]
-  [ PAYE Income Tax (Official government tax deduction) ]
-  [ Loan Deductions (Monthly repayment on salary advance) ]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
=  FINAL NET SALARY (Cash deposited to your bank account)
  1. Days Worked vs Official Days:
    If there are 22 working days in a month, and an employee was present or on approved paid leave for all 22 days, they receive 100% of their base salary. If they missed 2 days without approval, they are paid for 20 days.
  2. Overtime Pay:
    Hours worked on Sundays, holidays, or emergency night shifts recorded in the Daily Register are automatically multiplied by the company overtime rate and added to the paycheck.
  3. Statutory Deductions:
    • Pension (8%): Deducted and remitted directly into your registered Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) account for your future retirement.
    • PAYE Tax: Calculated strictly according to official Nigerian tax regulations after applying the statutory Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA).
  4. Loan Repayments:
    If you have an active salary advance, your agreed monthly installment is deducted automatically so you never have to remember to make manual payments.

4.4 Machine Servicing & Oil Changes (Maintenance)

Where to go: In the left menu, click Operations ➔ Maintenance (/operations/maintenance)

Dewatering pumps and generators run 24 hours a day. Missing an oil change causes catastrophic engine failure.

How Service Alerts Work:

  • Every time a site supervisor logs daily operating hours, the app counts down to the next service (every 250 hours for diesel engine oil and filters).
  • When a machine reaches 250 hours, an orange "Service Due" alert appears on the Operations dashboard.

How a Mechanic Fills a Service Work Order:

  1. Open the machine's maintenance card.
  2. Check off the completed tasks:
    • Engine oil drained and refilled (15W-40)
    • New primary and secondary fuel filters installed
    • Vacuum pump priming belt tension checked
    • Water pump impeller inspected
  3. Type any notes (e.g. "Replaced worn rubber seal on pump head").
  4. Click Complete Service & Reset Counter. The machine is certified safe to run for another 250 hours.

4.5 Logging Daily Diesel Fuel & Operating Hours

Step-by-Step: How to Design a Dewatering Layout with AI

Step 1: Upload Your Site Drawing

  1. Open the simulator and click AI Dewatering Assistant.
  2. Upload your architectural site drawing, CAD plan, or excavation drawing (as a PNG or JPG picture).

Step 2: Set the Scale (Calibrate Measurement)

  1. Click the Calibrate Scale tool.
  2. Click on two grid lines or structural building columns on the drawing where you know the real distance.
  3. Type in the real distance in meters (for example: 25 meters). The software now knows the exact real-world dimensions of the drawing.

Step 3: Trace Your Excavation Pit

  1. Click the Trace Pit tool.
  2. Click on each corner of your excavation pit on the drawing to outline the hole.
  3. Double-click the last corner to close the shape. The app shows you the exact length around the pit (perimeter) and square meters (area).

Step 4: Choose Your Site Settings

Fill in a few simple questions:

  • How deep is the excavation? (e.g. -6.5 meters).
  • Where is the natural ground water? (e.g. -1.2 meters).
  • Which side will trucks drive into the pit? Select North, South, East, or West, and set the ramp width (e.g. 6 meters). The software will make sure no pipes or pumps block the truck entrance ramp!
  • Pipe Size: Choose 6m Modular Pipes.

Step 5: Click Generate Layout!

Click the blue Generate Dewatering Blueprint button:

  • The app automatically places header pipes all the way around your excavation.
  • It inserts wellpoint riser pipes every 1 meter.
  • It places 6-inch diesel vacuum pumps in safe spots away from the truck ramp.
  • It runs discharge hoses to your water disposal channel.
  • Click 3D View to rotate and look at your deep excavation in realistic 3D!

Step 6: Export Your Pipe & Pump List (BOM)

Click Export Bill of Materials (BOM):

  • The app instantly gives you an exact count:
    • 24 pieces of 6-meter header pipes
    • 48 wellpoint risers and filters
    • 2 Sykes 6-inch diesel pumps
    • Couplings, bends, and layflat discharge hoses
  • Click Send to Waybill to immediately pass this list to the Yard Foreman so they can load the delivery truck!

8.1 Common Problems & Quick Fixes

Problem What Happened? How to Fix It
"I forgot my password" You mistyped or forgot your login password. On the sign-in screen, click "Forgot your password?" and check your email for the reset link, or contact your HR manager.
"Access Denied: Account Inactive" Your profile was set to inactive. Ask an administrator in Settings ➔ Users to check the Active Account box on your profile.
"I am on a remote site with no phone signal" No cellular or WiFi connection on site. Continue entering your daily attendance, site diary, and fuel logs normally. The app saves everything locally on your phone and will upload automatically as soon as you return to an area with signal.
"The screen didn't update after I saved" Your web browser is showing an old cached view. Press Ctrl + Shift + R on your computer keyboard to refresh the page, or pull down on your phone screen to reload.
"I cannot mark a task as Completed" The task has a "Pending Approval" restriction. This task involves spending money or moving machinery and requires your manager to sign off first. Ask your department head to approve it.
"When printing, the edges of the page are cut off" Your printer margins are set too wide. In the printer preview window, change Margins from "Default" to "None" and check the box for "Background Graphics".
